In your Tesla

  1. While parked, open this page in the car's browser and bookmark it.
  2. Pick a car, choose GPS drive and press Start engine. Say yes when the browser asks for location.
  3. Turn the media volume up and drive. The engine follows your real speed.
  4. Waiting at a light? Hold Rev.

How the sound is made

There are no recordings. Each engine is modelled live in the browser: every cylinder fires in its real order, and the pulses run through simulated headers, pipes and a muffler. Turbo whistle, supercharger whine and lift-off crackle sit on top.

A virtual gearbox picks gears from your speed, so you hear upshifts, downshifts and the limiter.

About the speed reading

Speed comes straight from the GPS receiver. Fixes arrive about once a second, so ElectricSound runs a small motion filter that predicts between fixes to keep the engine smooth and close to the car's own speedometer.

Your location stays in this browser. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account and nothing to pay.

ElectricSound is a hobby project and is not affiliated with Tesla or any car maker. Car names describe the sound profile each engine is modelled on, and specs are approximate. Sound plays through the cabin speakers like music. Set it up while parked and keep your eyes on the road.
